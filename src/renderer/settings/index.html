Claude Code

Buddy reacts to Claude Code's hooks. Connecting adds them to ~/.claude/settings.json and leaves the rest of the file untouched — your old copy is kept as settings.json.bak.

When to light

After you accept a request, Claude goes back to Working. Set it to Off to turn the strip off, or to Solid colour to keep a steady light. ▶ previews a pattern for 5 seconds.
